About these figures & sources

Counters accrue from cumulative totals since 1850 at present-day rates: carbon ~360,000 kg C/s (41.6 Gt CO2/yr); excess heat ~6.99×1011 kJ/s (Earth’s energy imbalance ~1.37 W/m², ≈11 Hiroshima bombs per second). Warming rises ~0.27°C/decade from ~1.44°C today.

“Stop today” reflects committed warming: CO2-only warming roughly stabilises (Zero Emissions Commitment ~0±0.3°C, IPCC AR6), but losing the ~0.5°C of aerosol “masking” unmasks a few tenths more. Lines are constant-rate extrapolations — illustrative, not IPCC scenarios.

Sources: Global Carbon Budget 2024 · IPCC AR6 WG1 · Berkeley Earth
