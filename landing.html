The climate crisis, ticking in real time.

A compact widget that shows carbon released, heat trapped, and global warming since 1850 — updating live. Drop it into any website, sidebar or footer in one line. It adapts to whatever space you give it.

What it shows

Three numbers that never stop moving

Every value is computed in the browser from published climate data — no servers, no cookies, no network calls.

Carbon released

Cumulative carbon emitted since 1850 — switch between kilograms and “Exxon Valdez loads”.

Extra heat trapped

Excess energy in the Earth system, shown in kilojoules or Hiroshima bombs (~11 every second).

Global warming

Warming above the pre-industrial baseline, plus 2050 & 2100 projections against the Paris limits.

Add it to your site

Two ways to embed — pick one, paste, done

Both are free and load from a global CDN. The widget is self-contained and won’t clash with your page’s styles.

Recommended

Web Component

One script tag + one element. Works in plain HTML, WordPress, React, anything.

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/matjanw/climate-ticker@1.0.0/climate-ticker.js"></script>
<climate-ticker></climate-ticker>

iframe

Best for locked-down site builders (Wix, Squarespace). The script lets it auto-resize to fit.

<iframe id="climate-ticker" src="https://matjanw.github.io/climate-ticker/widget.html"
        width="100%" height="640" loading="lazy" scrolling="no"
        style="border:0;max-width:720px;display:block;margin:0 auto"
        title="Climate Ticker"></iframe>
<script>addEventListener('message',function(e){
  if(e.data&&e.data.type==='climate-ticker:resize'){
    var f=document.getElementById('climate-ticker');
    if(f)f.style.height=e.data.height+'px';
  }
});</script>
  1. Copy one of the snippets above.
  2. Paste it into your page where you want the widget to appear (a sidebar makes it stack; a wide footer makes it a short banner).
  3. Optional — re-theme it with CSS variables, e.g. climate-ticker { --cs-bg:#fff; --cs-text:#111; }
